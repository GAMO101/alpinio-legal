2. Čo AlpinIO je

AlpinIO je appka pre bezpečnosť a rozhodovanie v horách, zameraná na Tatry. Zobrazuje lavínové bulletiny (SLP/HZS), počasie a turistickú mapu s navigáciou. Appka informuje — rozhodnutie v teréne robíte vy.

Pri prihlásení cez Google appka cez Firebase dostane vaše meno, email a URL profilovej fotky — ide o štandardný OAuth flow (appka od verzie September 2026 používa Firebase-vlastný webview OAuth, nie samostatný Google plugin; rozsah získaných údajov je rovnaký: email + profile).

3.2 Poloha

Appka žiada presnú polohu len na popredí ("while in use") — oprávnenie na polohu na pozadí appka nežiada (v AndroidManifest.xml je takéto oprávnenie len zakomentované, nie aktívne).

Poloha sa používa na:

  • zobrazenie vašej pozície na mape,
  • vycentrovanie počasia a lavínového bulletinu na vaše miesto,
  • výpočet pešej trasy a vyhľadávanie "v okolí",
  • výber najbližšieho z 3 výškových pásiem (údolie/stred/hrebeň) pri zobrazovaní počasia — GPS výška (Position.altitude) sa použije výhradne na vašom zariadení na tento výber a nikdy sa neposiela na náš backend.

Komu a čo z polohy odchádza:

  • Naše vlastné Cloud Functions (europe-west1, Belgicko) dostanú vaše súradnice, aby vypočítali, do ktorej ~5 km mapovej bunky patríte. Ukladá sa zdieľaná dátová bunka, nie história vašej osobnej polohy.
  • Pri plánovaní trasy appka posiela štart/cieľ/medzizastávky (presné súradnice) na náš vlastný self-hosted navigačný server (BRouter, Cloud Run, europe-west1). Tento server nemá vlastnú databázu používateľov ani úložisko trás a nevyžaduje autentifikáciu — endpoint je verejne dostupný. Google Cloud Run štandardne loguje technické metadáta HTTP požiadaviek (vrátane URL so súradnicami) rovnako, ako je popísané v bode 3.6 nižšie — tieto súradnice teda prechádzajú rovnakým štandardným infraštruktúrnym logovaním ako iné požiadavky.
  • Pri zobrazení mapy a vyhľadávaní miest appka posiela súradnice/vyhľadávací text priamo z vášho zariadenia na Mapbox (samostatná americká spoločnosť) — ide o súčasť použitia Mapbox SDK/API na vykreslenie mapy a vyhľadávanie. Pozri bod 5 nižšie k medzinárodnému prenosu.
  • Keď mapa zobrazuje vrstvu turistických chodníkov, appka sťahuje jej mapové dlaždice priamo z vášho zariadenia zo služby Waymarked Trails (waymarkedtrails.org). Služba tak dostane vašu IP adresu a súradnice zobrazenej časti mapy — nie vašu identitu ani účet.
  • Na Open-Meteo (poskytovateľ počasia) sa nikdy neposiela vaša poloha priamo — komunikuje s ním výhradne náš backend, a to len s hrubým stredom zdieľanej mapovej bunky, nikdy s vašou identitou.
  • Posledných 5 vyhľadaných miest sa ukladá len lokálne vo vašom zariadení (nie na našich serveroch) pre rýchlejšie opätovné vyhľadanie.
  • Pri SOS volaní appka len otvorí bežný telefónny vytáčač (112 / Horská záchranná služba) — vašu polohu neposiela záchranárom automaticky.

2. What AlpinIO is

AlpinIO is a mountain safety and decision-support app for the Tatras. It shows avalanche bulletins (SLP/HZS), weather, and a hiking map with navigation. The app informs — you make the decision in the field.

3. What data we collect, for what purpose, and on what legal basis

3.1 Account data

When you register (email/password or Google sign-in) we store: email address, username, display name, phone number (optional), profile photo (optional), country (chosen from a list, not derived from your location), app language preference, account timestamps, and the timestamp and version of this policy you acknowledged at registration.

If you sign in with Google, Firebase receives your name, email, and profile photo URL from Google as part of a standard OAuth flow (since September 2026 the app uses Firebase's own webview-based OAuth instead of a separate Google plugin; the data scope is the same: email + profile).

PurposeLegal basis
Account creation and management, sign-in Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — the app requires an account in V1, it doesn't work without one
Record that you acknowledged this policy Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — demonstrating that we informed you about the processing (GDPR Art. 5(2))

Email and username are required. Phone and photo are optional — not providing them does not limit any feature of the app.

3.2 Location

The app requests precise, foreground-only ("while in use") location — it does not request background location (the permission is only commented out, not active, in AndroidManifest.xml).

Your location is used to:

  • show your position on the map,
  • center weather and the avalanche bulletin on your location,
  • calculate hiking routes and search "nearby",
  • pick the nearest of 3 elevation bands (valley/mid/ridge) when showing weather — your GPS altitude (Position.altitude) is used purely on your device for this and is never sent to our backend.

Where your location actually goes:

  • Our own Cloud Functions (europe-west1, Belgium) receive your coordinates to resolve which ~5 km map cell you're in. We store the shared cell data, not a history of your personal location.
  • When planning a route, the app sends start/end/waypoint coordinates to our own self-hosted routing server (BRouter, Cloud Run, europe-west1). That server has no user database or route storage of its own and requires no authentication — the endpoint is publicly reachable. Google Cloud Run logs standard HTTP request metadata by default (including the request URL, which contains the coordinates) in the same way described in section 3.6 below — these coordinates pass through the same standard infrastructure logging as any other request.
  • When rendering the map or searching for places, the app sends coordinates/search text directly from your device to Mapbox (a separate US company) — this is inherent to using the Mapbox SDK/API for map rendering and place search. See section 5 below on international transfer.
  • When the map shows the hiking trails layer, the app downloads its map tiles directly from your device from Waymarked Trails (waymarkedtrails.org). The service therefore receives your IP address and the coordinates of the visible map area — not your identity or account.
  • Your location is never sent directly to Open-Meteo (our weather provider) — only our backend talks to it, and only with the coarse center of a shared map cell, never your identity.
  • Your last 5 searched places are stored only locally on your device (not on our servers) so you can find them again faster.
  • During an SOS call, the app only opens your phone's normal dialer (112 / Mountain Rescue) — it does not transmit your location to rescuers automatically.
PurposeLegal basis
Map, weather, avalanche bulletin for your location, route planning, nearby search Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — this is core app functionality; these specific features do not work without location

3.3 Feedback submissions

If you voluntarily submit a report in the app (bug, suggestion, "this data looks wrong", other), we store:

  • your uid (account identifier),
  • the report type and text,
  • an optional contact email you type in yourself,
  • technical context for debugging: app version, Android version, device model, app language, your last map cell (regionId — not your precise location), and the last captured error code.
PurposeLegal basis
Handling your report, debugging Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — handling a report you chose to send and fixing bugs in the app. You can object to this processing and ask for a specific report to be deleted via the contact email.

3.4 Security / anti-abuse signal

The app obtains a Google Play Integrity token (via Firebase App Check), which the Firebase libraries attach to requests to Firebase services. This makes it possible to verify that a request comes from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app — a standard anti-abuse measure. It involves an exchange of integrity signals between your device and Google, governed by Google's own policies.

PurposeLegal basis
Protecting the backend against abuse/fraudulent requests Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — security of network and information systems

3.5 What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK (no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics).
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs.
  • No background location.
  • No payments — the app is free, no in-app purchases.

3.6 Standard infrastructure logging

Like virtually any cloud-hosted app, our backend (Google Firebase/Cloud Functions and our self-hosted routing server on Cloud Run) automatically logs technical request metadata (e.g. IP address, timestamp, request path/parameters, error messages) for security and debugging, per Google Cloud's standard policies. AlpinIO does not configure this separately or use it for profiling.

These technical records are kept for 30 days. Separate mandatory audit records of administrative changes to the project are kept by Google Cloud for 400 days.

9. Automated decision-making

The app does not make any automated decision with a legal effect on you. The app also internally computes its own risk score (MRI), but this is not shown anywhere in V1 (calibration isn't finished yet) and does not drive any automated action.
